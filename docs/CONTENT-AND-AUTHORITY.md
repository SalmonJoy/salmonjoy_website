### Month 2: How to evaluate a RAG chatbot

- Proposed slug: evaluate-rag-chatbot; topic: Engineering; reader: developers and technical leads.
- Search intent: choose useful evaluation criteria for a retrieval-based assistant.
- Outline: define the task; build representative questions and expected sources; evaluate retrieval separately from answer support; test missing/conflicting/stale evidence; measure latency; review failures; record changes and rerun comparable tests.
- Original asset: a blank evaluation worksheet with question, expected evidence, retrieved source, supported answer, uncertainty and reviewer notes.
- Use an explicitly illustrative mini test set. Do not claim the existing 88% grounding result is a universal benchmark or manufacture its missing evaluation method.
- Consult current primary evaluation and retrieval documentation. Include limits: small test sets, reviewer variation and changes in source material.
- Link to the first RAG explainer, enterprise RAG case and engineering training page.

### Month 3: Designing hands-on AI faculty development

- Proposed slug: hands-on-ai-faculty-development; topic: Education; reader: faculty and programme coordinators.
- Search intent: plan an AI learning activity with visible reasoning and assessment.
- Outline: define the learning outcome; select a suitable baseline; prepare approved examples; compare supported and unsupported answers; discuss attribution; use an assessment rubric; adapt the activity to the department.
- Original asset: a fictional activity brief and rubric with source quality, reasoning, uncertainty and reflection. No accreditation or certification claim.
- Use actual workshop photographs only with their correct captions. Ask Salmon for a concrete teaching observation before adding first-person anecdotes.
- Sources: institution policies plus current primary educational guidance. Identify which parts are recommendations rather than institutional rules.
- Link to /training/faculty-development/, /about/ and relevant genuine workshop evidence.

### Month 3: Measuring outcomes of team AI training

- Proposed slug: measure-ai-training-outcomes; topic: Business AI; reader: HR, L&D and team leaders.
- Search intent: evaluate learning and workplace adoption without confusing attendance with results.
- Outline: define a task; record the baseline; apply the same review criteria; include checking and revision time; compare equivalent outputs; follow up on adoption; document confounders and limits.
- Original asset: a before/after review template with blank values and a worked fictional example explicitly labelled as such. Do not manufacture an ROI percentage.
- Separate participation, satisfaction, demonstrated skill and work outcomes. Show why time saved without quality checks can be misleading.
- Link to the training-selection guide, business training page and training hub.
